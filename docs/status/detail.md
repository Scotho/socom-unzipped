# The status map, in detail

![The status map](map.svg)

Generated by `python -m tools_py.status_map` from `docs/status/status.toml`; do not edit by hand (edit the
data file and regenerate; `--check` fails while this file or `map.svg` is stale). Statuses are read from
`docs/KNOWN.md`, which wins on any disagreement. *Working* counts towards the percentages; *Partial* and
*Pending* do not.

**The status rule.** The sources are `docs/KNOWN.md`, `docs/LATER.md`, `docs/HAZARDS.md`, `docs/BACKLOG.md` and
the open GitHub issues it lists, whatever their milestone; `docs/KNOWN.md` wins on any disagreement. *Working* (ok):
works in the game with nothing open recorded against it; being unverified against a console does not make a row
partial on its own, and the note says so where it applies. *Partial*: an open defect or a recorded divergence,
whatever its graded impact, or a stub that answers the game without the real behaviour. *Pending*: not working, or
not modelled. An unproven candidate (a suspected cause nobody has shown) is named in the note and does not count.

**What the libraries panel leaves out.** Only the loader `SCUS_972.75` is counted. The Sony code linked into the
game image itself is not: the libnet EE wrappers (0x245690-0x2484a0, research/10), the lgAud headset client, the
989snd `snd_*` client and the `sceInet*` calls, among others. The loader's own code (its `main()`, the "mp_net"
BSD-socket shim of research/10) is the game's, and zlib 1.1.4 is a third-party library; both are left out too.

**The hardware weights** are a rough share of the work each component asks of a PS2 recompilation and its runtime
(the CPU 5; the kernel, GS, VIF1/VU1 and SPU2 3; most others 2; the timers, USB and the HDD 1). They are
judgement calls, as in the map this one follows, and they decide only the size of a rectangle and the hardware
percentage.

## Feature completion

The badges marked † are computed here from the category scores of the 2026-10-11 feature completion audit
(commit 4174dbc3b, unmerged). Its scores are judgement calls drawn from `docs/KNOWN.md`, the plans and the code;
KNOWN wins on any disagreement. The audit was scored before Sprint 18 closed, so two of its rows are re-scored
here from KNOWN section 1's row "The launcher's PCSX2 client reaches our lobby from a clean home (2026-10-12)";
the category notes below say which. By the owner's ruling of 2026-10-12 the status map counts the recomp, the game
and our server only; the audit's other two categories, and its second headline figure that averages them, are
left out.
