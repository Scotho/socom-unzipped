# Sprint 13 Implementation Plan — "nothing carried twice"

> Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-sprint-13-nothing-carried-twice-design.md`. The master list it came from:
> `docs/audits/2026-09-25-project-audit.md` (its six reports beside it). Opened 2026-09-25 night by the local
> controller (session socom-pc-6c) on the owner's instruction; branch `sprint-13` off `main` once Sprint 12's PR
> merges (until then the opening commits ride on a topic branch and rebase onto it). The loop runs this plan in
> `docs/LOOP_PROMPT.md`'s shape: one task at a time, a failing test first, review by a fresh agent, the lock for
> every build and run, the owner away.

## Global Constraints

- **Order:** milestones V, R, H, C, U, S, N, O (S13-R1) — visible defects first, then the record, then what costs
  every night. A task out of order needs a ruling that says what it jumped and why.
- **The lock:** one build or launch at a time (`scripts/loop_lock.sh run` / `run_detached.sh`); the owner is away on
  this sprint's nights (S13-R2), so lock-bound runs are allowed at night, never two at once, never a game window when
  the owner has said they are at the machine. At most two C++-building agents at once.
- **A failing test first**, unittest only; RED and GREEN pasted; the gate 3/3 with PINS MATCH on the rebuilt exe
  before a runtime change is called done; `./build.sh runtime` before any gate when the runtime changed.
- **Commit with an explicit pathspec**, subject `type(scope): what and why`, this session's trailer, the leak check
  in the hooks; never `--no-verify`; push `origin sprint-13`; check CI (a docs-only push is `skipped` after H1 lands,
  `success` with the build skipped before it — read the run, not the word).
- **Every claim names its artefact:** an issue closes with the artefact in its closing comment; a document sentence
  about the tree cites a commit, a stamp or a test; a "merged as vX" names a tag that exists (R268).
- **The record's ceilings (R268)** hold from R1 onward: the sprint file, HANDOFF §2, STATUS's state block and
  HUMAN_TASKS stay under the sizes R1 sets; this file's Log is the sprint's live state, newest first.
- **Nothing that is the owner's** (§3 of the audit) is performed; a draft is prepared and an `O<n>` line updated.
- **Reviews:** every task by a fresh agent that did not write it, findings `file:line`, fix rounds to a clean
  re-review; the diffs under `.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-25-sprint-13/` (git-ignored).
