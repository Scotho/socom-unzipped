# Sprint 15 Implementation Plan — "borrowed confidence" (PROPOSED)

> Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-sprint-15-borrowed-confidence-design.md`. The origin:
> `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-25-borrowed-confidence-cloud-handoff.md`, the cloud research handoff that never
> ran, rewritten here for the owner's machine (no cloud session from 2026-09-26). Proposed 2026-09-26 while Sprint 13
> is open and Sprint 14 is proposed; **opens when Sprint 14's Task 99 closes** (Sprint 14 D8) — branch `sprint-15`
> off `main` at the Sprint 14 merge. The loop runs this plan with the `loop-iteration` skill Sprint 14 I2 lands
> (or `docs/LOOP_PROMPT.md`'s shape if it did not): one task at a time, a failing test first, review by a fresh
> agent, the lock for every build and run, the merged chain as the gate unit.

**Goal:** every subsystem we replaced in PS2Recomp has a row with its confidence and the experiment that would
settle it; every credible outside answer to a non-Proven row has a path and a validation; at least three of them
are tried to a recorded outcome, the two the owner named first.

**Architecture:** two research notes (class S, dated) written by agents from the tree and from `gh`, reviewed by a
fresh agent against their own commands; trial tasks written into this plan at a checkpoint from the survey's
shortlist, each in its own agent worktree with a RED test, a named measurement and a stop rule; every lock-bound
step through the merged chain at a quiet window; KNOWN moved with the evidence.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 unittest, Git Bash via `tools_py/tests/shell.BASH`, `gh` against `ran-j/PS2Recomp` and its
forks (read-only), the existing `vu1_replay`, `ps2xTest`, `tools_py/parity/gate.py`, `audio_dips.py`,
`audio_parity.py`, `scripts/loop_lock.sh`, `scripts/agent_worktree.sh`, the Sprint 14 guards and skills.

## Global Constraints

- **Order:** R, X, T (with V as needed), L at the end of each milestone (D5's boxes). A task out of order needs a
  ruling that says what it jumped and why.
- **The register decides the scope:** no survey entry without a register row; no trial without a shortlist entry;
  no shortlist entry without a validation in our terms and the number today.
- **A failing test first**, unittest only; RED and GREEN pasted into the Log; a trial's measurement named, with
  today's value, before the trial's branch exists.
- **The lock:** one build or launch at a time through `scripts/loop_lock.sh run` / `run_detached.sh`; at most two
  building agents (Sprint 14 W1 enforces it); the lock-bound steps of a trial run at night through the merged chain
  (`scripts/parity/merged_chain.sh`, Sprint 14 W2), never a gate per trial; a new capture at a quiet window only.
- **Nothing derived from the disc enters the tree:** not a byte of `game/`, `recomp/output*/`, `logs/` or a
  recording; a count from the generated code is cited with its command. The private inputs are on this machine;
  nothing fetches them.
- **Commit with an explicit pathspec**, subject `type(scope): what and why` under 120 characters, the trailer, the
  leak check in the hooks; never `--no-verify`; push `origin sprint-15`; `gh run list --commit` after a code push.
- **Every claim names its artefact;** a research note's numbers name their commands; a disagreement with a prior
  verdict (research/42, 63) is stated with the evidence, never by editing the note.
- **KNOWN wins;** a finding that moves a row moves it in KNOWN in the commit that carries the evidence.
- **Rulings from the global counter only** (Sprint 14 D5); each moved default gets one at the open; no `S15-R` names.
- **Reviews:** every task by a fresh agent that did not write it (the `reviewer` definition, Sprint 14 G4), findings
  `file:line`, fix rounds to a clean re-review; diffs under `.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-26-sprint-15/` (git-ignored).
  A research note's review checks the claims against the commands it names.
- **Nothing that is the owner's** is performed; O rows gain a line, never an act; no upstream PR is filed (D3).
- **No cloud session.** Every task runs on this machine in an agent worktree; `gh` reads are host work too.

```
## Task T<n>: <the approach, in one line>  — settles register row <name>
Adoption path: TAKE | REIMPLEMENT | BORROW THE IDEA. Source: <repo, path, licence file>.
Measurement: <tool and command>; today: <number>; adopted if: <number>; stop rule: <result that ends it>.
Files: <ours>. Lock-bound steps: <which, through the merged chain>.
- [ ] Step 1: the RED test (<file>, the case, the failing run pasted).

- [ ] Step 3: the merged chain at a quiet window: recomp if the image changed (D2), runtime, suites, the gate 3/3,

      TRIED, NOT ADOPTED (the number and the reason in the register and the Log; the branch reverted or left
      unmerged with "throwaway" in its last subject). Either way, one line in the Log.
```
